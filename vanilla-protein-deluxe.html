Vanilla Protein (Deluxe)

Mostly milk, double the usual protein powder, and vanilla flavor. Low fat, low sugar, and high protein. Works with dairy milk and whey protein too, or fully vegan with soy milk and soy protein.

Yield: 1 Creami pint, about 659 g (2 servings)  |  Prep: 15 min  |  Freeze: 24 hours  |  Program: Lite Ice Cream

Per 340 g serving: 300 cal, 29.9 g protein, 50.9 g carbs (6.6 g sugars), 4.6 g fat, 1.0 g salt

Ingredients

Wet

  • 500 ml soy milk 1.6% sugar-free (about 2 cups + 1 tbsp) - or any preferred milk around 2% fat
  • 15 g glycerin / vegetable glycerine, E422 (about 1 tbsp)
  • 10 g brandy or vodka 40% (about 2 tsp) - or 8 g extra glycerin for an alcohol-free version

Dry

  • 40 g soy protein isolate, unsweetened and unflavored (about 1 oz + 2 1/4 tsp)
  • 40 g SweEX, erythritol + xylitol 3:2 (about 1 oz + 2 1/4 tsp)
  • 20 g inulin (about 1 tbsp + 1 tsp)
  • 20 g skim milk powder, SMP (about 1 tbsp + 1 tsp) - optional, skip for a vegan version
  • 10 g Salty Stability blend (about 2 tsp) - substitute 1 g guar gum + 0.3 g xanthan gum + 0.3 g salt
  • 4 g vanilla bean powder (about 3/4 tsp) - or 7 g vanilla extract or paste

Fill to MAX line

  • About 5 drops vanilla flavor drops, sucralose - to taste

Mix-ins

  • 15 g dried sour cherries, pitted (about 1 tbsp) - chopped, added as a mix-in
  • 15 g freeze-dried strawberry slices (about 1 tbsp) - crumbled, added as a mix-in

Directions

  1. Add the "wet" ingredients to the empty Creami tub.
  2. Weigh and mix the dry ingredients - easiest by adding them to a jar with a secure lid and shaking vigorously.
  3. Pour the dry mix into the tub and quickly use an immersion blender on full speed to homogenize everything.
  4. Let the blender run until the thickeners are properly hydrated, up to 1-2 minutes. Or wait that long and blend again.
  5. Add the remaining ingredients up to the MAX line and stir with a spoon.
  6. For better results, let the base age in the fridge with the lid on for a few hours or overnight. This helps flavor development and gum hydration, especially with unheated bases.
  7. Freeze for 24 hours with the lid on, then spin as usual. Flatten any humps before spinning. Use the Lite Ice Cream program.
  8. Process with RE-SPIN if it is not creamy enough after the first spin - with this recipe a second spin is often not needed.
  9. Process with MIX-IN after adding the mix-ins evenly. Add partial amounts into a hole going down to the bottom, and fold the ice cream over, building pockets of mix-ins.

Notes

  • Vegan and dairy-free: leave out the skim milk powder and the recipe is soy milk and soy protein only.
  • Rating from the source: thick and creamy.
  • Salty Stability (10 g) is 7.3 g inulin, 1.2 g glycerol monostearate (GMS, E471), 0.6 g tylose powder (CMC, E466), 0.4 g guar gum (E412), 0.33 g salt, 0.13 g xanthan gum (E415).
  • FPDF / PAC: 32.12 (target 20-30). Protein / energy ratio: 39.79% (ok = 12%, high = 20%). Milk solids non-fat: 76.0 g, 11.5% (target 7-11%).
  • Net carbs: 25.9 g total - about 5.2 g per 132 g serving, or 8.6 g per 220 g serving.
