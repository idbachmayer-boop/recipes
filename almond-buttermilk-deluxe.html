Almond Buttermilk (Deluxe)

Ice cream based on almond butter and amaretto as the main flavor, with buttermilk and unsweetened soy milk to keep the sugar lower. An emulsifier is used to properly integrate the nut butter.

Yield: 1 Creami tub, about 720 g (2 servings)  |  Prep: 20 min  |  Freeze: 24 hours  |  Program: Lite Ice Cream, scrape down, re-spin

Per 360 g serving: 344 cal, 16.5 g protein, 50.3 g carbs (17.4 g sugars), 13.1 g fat, 0.7 g salt

Ingredients

Prep

  • 150 ml soy milk 1.6% sugar-free (about 5 fl oz) - or any preferred milk around 2% fat
  • 40 g creamy almond butter (about 1 oz + 2 1/4 tsp)
  • 15 g skim milk powder, SMP (about 1 tbsp)
  • 2 g glycerol monostearate, GMS / E471 (about 1/2 tsp)

Wet

  • 350 ml buttermilk 1% (about 1 cup + 3 fl oz + 1 tbsp)
  • 10 g glycerin / vegetable glycerine, E422 (about 2 tsp)
  • 25 ml amaretto 28% (about 1 tbsp + 2 tsp)

Dry

  • 40 g SweEX, erythritol + xylitol 3:2 (about 1 oz + 2 1/4 tsp)
  • 15 g Salty Stability blend, unsweetened ICSv2 (about 1 tbsp)

Fill to MAX line

  • 73 ml buttermilk 1% (about 5 tbsp)
  • About 10 drops cookies and cream flavor drops, stevia - to taste

Mix-ins

  • 25 g dark chocolate 70% (about 1 tbsp + 2 tsp) - chopped or shaved, added as a mix-in
  • 10 g almond slivers (about 2 tsp) - added as a mix-in or topping
  • 14 g amarettini, about 10 pieces (about 2 3/4 tsp) - added as a mix-in or topping

Topping options

  • 5 ml molasses (about 1 tsp) - added as a topping

Directions

  1. Microwave the soy milk and almond butter to 70 C / 158 F.
  2. Mix the skim milk powder and GMS together, then blend them into the hot milk.
  3. Add the rest of the "wet" ingredients.
  4. Weigh and mix the dry ingredients - easiest by adding them to a jar with a secure lid and shaking vigorously.
  5. Pour into the tub and quickly use an immersion blender on full speed to homogenize everything.
  6. Let the blender run until the thickeners are properly hydrated, up to 1-2 minutes. Or wait that long and blend again.
  7. Add the remaining ingredients up to the MAX line and stir with a spoon.
  8. For better results, let the base age in the fridge with the lid on for a few hours or overnight. This helps flavor development and gum hydration, especially with unheated bases.
  9. Freeze for 24 hours with the lid on, then spin as usual. Flatten any humps before spinning. Use the Lite Ice Cream program with a scrape-down.
  10. Process with RE-SPIN if it is not creamy enough after the first spin.
  11. Process with MIX-IN after adding the mix-ins evenly. Add partial amounts into a hole going down to the bottom, and fold the ice cream over, building pockets of mix-ins.

Notes

  • An emulsifier is used to properly integrate the nut butter. Dissolving the GMS, skim milk powder and almond butter in heated soy milk improved the texture a lot - with that, only one spin was needed and no re-spins.
  • Variant - Peanut Buttermilk: use peanut butter instead of almond butter.
  • Optional PB cream mix-in for the variant: 50 ml cream 32%, 20 g peanut butter powder, 10 g trehalose, 0.2 g salt. Combine to a smooth paste and add as a mix-in. Adds about 268 kcal, 18.8 g fat, 13.9 g carbs, 12.3 g sugar, 10.9 g protein per 80 g.
  • Another variation from the source: add 100 g cottage cheese, process on FroYo, and use dark chocolate flakes as a mix-in.
  • Salty Stability (15 g) is 11.0 g inulin, 1.8 g glycerol monostearate (GMS, E471), 0.9 g tylose powder (CMC, E466), 0.6 g guar gum (E412), 0.5 g salt, 0.2 g xanthan gum (E415).
  • FPDF / PAC: 31.04 (target 20-30). Protein / energy ratio: 19.20%. Milk solids non-fat: 56.1 g, 7.8% (target 7-11%).
  • Net carbs: 44.5 g total - about 8.9 g per 144 g serving, or 14.8 g per 240 g serving.
