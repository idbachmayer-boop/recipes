Cookies & Cream

A classic vanilla base ice cream mixed with oreo cookie mix ins. Tip: Remove the cream to cut the calories and increase the crunch factor with extra wafers. — A high-protein Ninja Creami recipe by HadawaysHealthyHabits on The Pint Lab.

Yield: 1 pint  |  Prep: 10 min  |  Freeze: 24 hours

Per pint: 330 calories, 38 g protein, 44 g carbs, 6 g fat

Ingredients

  • 1 scoop Vanilla Ice Cream protein powder
  • 1 cup Fairlife Fat Free Milk -240ml
  • 3/4 cup Unsweetened Vanilla Almond Milk - Blue Diamond - 180ml
  • 1 tbsp Sugar-free Vanilla Pudding 7 gr
  • 1 tbsp Allulose - 10 gr
  • 1/8 teaspoon Xanthan Gum
  • 1/8 teaspoon McCormick Vanilla Extract
  • Pinch Salt
  • 4 Cookies Oreo's Cream Removed - 4 sandwiches (8 Cookies)

Instructions

  1. Mix Dry: Whisk dry ingredients in pint: Protein powder, pudding, allulose, salt, and any other additional dry ingredients like cocoa powder. Get a good mix before adding wet ingredients.
  2. Mix Wet: Add wet ingredients into the pint. Make sure to stir well. I use a butter knife and scrape all the dry residue off the side. I know you can use wet first, to mitigate this, but sometimes the milk amounts need to vary a bit in order NOT to go above the fill line, and this is the best way in my experience.
  3. Blend: Blend with an immersion blender until smooth or pour mixture into a blender and run on lowest setting for 5 seconds. Blenders vary; you might have to tinker with this a bit. This will add air into the Creami mix. When you pour it back into the pint, it usually comes a little above the fill line. This is ok if your original pre-blended mix was below; you’re fine. The air will settle out. If it’s to the rim its too much to settle. It’s important to drink it down to the fill line before freezing.
  4. Freeze: Freeze pints for 24 hours. I have pulled pints as early as 12 hours, and it messes with the texture, making them soupy. I do NOT recommend less than 24-hour freeze time.
  5. Spin the Pint: Run the Creami on the Ice Cream mix unless noted in the recipe. Specifically, sherbet and some of the heavy mix-in flavors that require hand stirring/folding in the mix-ins. Alternatively, run on Lite Ice Cream or want a fluffier consistency like soft serve (McDonald's ice cream).
  6. Melt Side Ice: Remove pint and run under warm water for 15 seconds to melt the ice crystals on the side and bottom.
  7. Add mix-ins: If using mix-ins, dig and eat a hole OFF CENTERED all the way to the bottom of the pint! Add your mix-ins and run the pint again on “Mix-in” flavor. By not placing the hole directly in the middle, we avoid the mix-ins getting pulverized by our blade. This will give you small chunks.
  8. Tip: Keep your mix-ins cold (Refrigerated or Frozen).
  9. Optional: Sometimes you need a re-spin. In my experience, this is usually only if I eat half a pint and save the rest. It will have a powder/clumpy texture. If so, run the re-spin setting for the desired consistency.
  10. Add Toppings: Add toppings if desired and enjoy!
