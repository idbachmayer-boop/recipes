Cottage Cheese Protein Ice Cream

A cottage cheese base for the Ninja Creami - blend it first if you want a smooth, curd-free texture.

Yield: 1 pint

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups cottage cheese
  • 4 oz milk or milk substitute
  • 1 1/2 scoops protein powder (optional)
  • Sweetener to taste (optional)
  • 2 tbsp instant pudding mix (optional, for creaminess - or sub 10g unflavored collagen powder, or 1/4-1/2 tsp guar or xanthan gum)

Instructions

  1. Blend or process the cottage cheese first if a smooth (non-curd) texture is preferred.
  2. Mix all ingredients directly in the Ninja Creami container.
  3. Let rest uncovered 5-10 minutes.
  4. Cover and freeze overnight.
  5. Let thaw 10 minutes (or run hot water over the container), then run the "Lite Ice Cream" cycle.
  6. Add 1-2 oz milk or milk substitute and hit "Re-Spin" (repeat if needed).
  7. Add mix-ins and hit "Mix-In."
